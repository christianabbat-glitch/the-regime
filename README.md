# The Regime

A board game about surviving Stalin's Great Terror.

Play it at https://christianabbat-glitch.github.io/the-regime/

## The current playtest (the reset)

`index.html` is the reset version: secret owners, a corridor where men come forward, five department meetings (Propaganda, NKVD, Industry, Red Army, Judiciary) run with split agenda/response cards, the loudest-voice rule, the Lubyanka and its trials, the sharashka, the Gulag queue, the White Sea Canal and exile. The blue NKVD deck is the clock. You play against computer players; there is no declared winner yet.

## The classic version

`classic/index.html` is the earlier chips-and-fuses game, including its online play (Firebase rooms): https://christianabbat-glitch.github.io/the-regime/classic/
